1 · 5-minute orientation (the only context you need)

The vehicle & the two formulas

  • Vehicle: NYS OGS HBITS (Group 73012 / Award 23158 → Solicitation 23311). Non-negotiated, fixed NTE bill rates, firm for each placement's life. 31 titles × 4 skills × 3 regions. 5-yr + 2-yr option; bid opens 7/1/2026.
  • The mechanic that governs everything — the MSP price filter: for 10–40 candidates the MSP forwards only the lowest ~50% of bill rates (+1% buffer) before any technical review; 6–10 → 5 lowest; ≤5 → all. At ~33 bidders that's ~16–17 forwarded.
  • Formula 1.0 (Baseline): cost-plus. Bill = Wage × (1+M). Wage = historical 2021 anchor × CPI multipliers (de-esc 0.8417 → bid-yr 1.0381 → 2029 1.0716). Lands us in the expensive third (~#26/33); covers 30 titles (omits Business Analyst).
  • Formula 1.1 (Rank positioning): place our rate at target rank #19 of 33 (18 below, 14 above), computed per region from the live competitor field. 356/372 roles hit #19 exactly; 16 fall to #18 on boundary ties. Covers all 31 titles.
  • The headline tension: #19 sits in the upper price half → on high-volume roles it is filtered out before evaluation. This is the first thing to resolve.

3 · The first 60 minutes — decision agenda

  • 0–10′ Benchmark integrity. Are stale (pre-2025, un-escalated) competitor rates a fair basis, or do we CPI-normalize the field to 2026 before ranking? (Materially shifts every v1.1 price.)
  • 10–25′ Target-rank strategy. Flat #19 vs tiered by role: commodity/high-volume titles aim ≤ filter cut-off (~#16); scarce/filter-bypassing titles can sit higher for margin. Define the tiers and the rank per tier.
  • 25–40′ Cost build & margin floor. Validate fringe/OH/G&A/fee; set a minimum acceptable net margin and a markup band (margin vs. wage-competitiveness for recruiting).
  • 40–50′ Escalation & decay. CPI (2.3–3.8%) trails IT wage inflation (3.5–5.5%). Decide protective posture and the multi-year repricing/monitoring rule.
  • 50–60′ Lock v1.2 parameters. Convert the calls above into a concrete parameter set; capture owners + open items in the worksheet and export.

6 · Open questions where your judgment is decisive

  • ① Filter vs. margin: what rank reliably clears the MSP cut-off on high-volume roles, and what margin does that imply at our cost build?
  • ② Tiering rule: by what signal do we classify a title as commodity vs. scarce — historical response volume, closure counts, BLS supply, or judgment?
  • ③ Stale-rate normalization: if we CPI-lift incumbents to 2026, does our #19 placement move materially, and is that defensible in a protest?
  • ④ Wage floor compliance: at higher markups the pass-through wage drops — where's the recruiting break-point by region/role?
  • ⑤ 10-year decay: what repricing/escalation posture protects margin given CPI-vs-wage divergence on a fixed-rate vehicle?
  • ⑥ Win-rate evidence: can the 2023–25 closure data calibrate the rank↔win-probability curve to replace the assumed #19?
