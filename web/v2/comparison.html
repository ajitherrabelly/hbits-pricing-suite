The Fundamental Difference

Best Pegasus V1 Strategy: Fix market rank (target #19) and let margin float. Results: 1.2% net margin, unstable profitability.
Best Pegasus V2.0 Strategy: Fix margin at 35-38% (KBI formula) and let rank float (#21). Results: 37.3% net margin, 30× profit improvement, market-leading consistency.

The Formula Difference

V1.1 Equation

bill = rank_target_bill
wage = bill / (1 + 0.30)
margin = leftover

You choose rank, solve for bill, then back into wage. Margin is whatever's left.

V2.0 Equation

wage = sourced_cost
bill = wage × 2.10
margin = locked in

You source the wage, apply 110% markup, ship it. Margin is guaranteed.

Why V2 Works

1. The KBI Insight

Knowledge Builders Inc. (KBI) is the #1 HBITS vendor with 317 wins and $160.5M in revenue (2023-2025). They use exactly V2's formula:

  • Fixed 110% markup (constant across all 372 job combos)
  • Lowest-cost wages in the field (rank #6 of 32)
  • Bill rank #21 (median — not cheap, not expensive)
  • 38.5% net margin (same as V2)

Key finding: KBI does NOT win on price. They win on incumbency, fulfillment volume, and customer relationships — while sitting in the median price lane and keeping fat margins.

2. V1's False Premise

V1.1 was built on the assumption: "MSPs award to the cheapest 50% of bidders."

Reality (from backtest of 1,488 real awards):

  • Winners in the cheapest 50%: 49.6%
  • Winners in the expensive half: 50.4% (a coin flip)
  • Winners who were the cheapest bidder: 0.2%
  • KBI's 295 wins at median rank #21: from the expensive half

Verdict: Price is not the driver. V1's "be cheap" strategy is falsified.

3. The Margin Math

V1 tries to hit a transparent rank (#19) by solving backwards: rank → bill → wage → margin. This produces a thin margin as a leftover.

V2 locks in the margin first (35-38%, proven sustainable), sources a wage, applies a fixed markup, and lands wherever in the rank distribution. The rank is an output, not a target.

Key Takeaways

✅ What V2 Solves

  • Margin parity with KBI — locked-in 35-38% vs. V1's 1.2%
  • Competitive bill positioning — rank #21 (median), not cheap but not expensive
  • Scalable profitability — margin doesn't erode with volume
  • Simple decision rule — "source cheapest wage, apply 110%, ship"
  • Wage escalation advantage — bill grows with wage, margin stays locked

⚠️ What V2 Does NOT Solve

  • Win volume vs KBI — KBI wins 295 awards; V2 model predicts ~65-73 wins. The ~227-win gap is non-price moat (incumbency, fulfillment, relationships). That's an operations program, not a pricing change.
  • Customer relationship switching costs — price won't overcome established vendor stickiness
