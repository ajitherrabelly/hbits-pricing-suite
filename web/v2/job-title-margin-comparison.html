Strategic Recommendation

Apply the fixed markup uniformly across all 31 HBITS job titles — do not title-tier the markup.
  • ✅ 35.5%–38.5% net margin on every title — lands in the high-markup tier with KBI & Genesys
  • ✅ +22 to +27 points over the low-markup tier (MVP, Sligo, Panha) on every role
  • ✅ Operational simplicity — one rule across 372 cells (31 titles × 4 levels × 3 regions)
  • ✅ Title-mix flexibility — chase whatever titles the MSP releases without re-pricing
  • ✅ Proven economics — mirrors KBI's profile (317 wins, $160.5M revenue, 38.5% margin)

Bottom line: The market has two margin tiers, and discipline decides which one you're in. Best Pegasus V2.0's fixed markup keeps it in the top tier on every title — beside the proven winner, far above the discounters.
