Hourly bill rate ($/hr) for 14 HBITS roles across all six players — Best Pegasus V2.0, KBI, Genesys, MVP, Sligo & Panha. Best Pegasus bills are the 2026 V2 quotes from the rate trajectory; competitor bills are the live HBITS field (Region 1). Switch skill level with the tabs.

What this shows: unlike the margin view (where vendors split into two flat tiers), bill rates rise with seniority and scarcity for everyone. Best Pegasus V2.0 tracks KBI almost exactly — both price wage × 2.10 off the same low sourced-wage floor, so the cyan and yellow lines sit on top of each other. The low-markup vendors (MVP, Sligo, Panha) pay higher wages, so their bills are not systematically cheaper — they simply keep less margin.
