Key Insights

1. KBI's Margin Dominance

KBI's 37.3% average margin is 5.5 percentage points higher than MVP (#2). This 14% margin advantage compounds over the portfolio, translating to:

  • Higher profit per win (enables reinvestment)
  • Capital for better recruiting and technology
  • Sustainable growth without margin compression

2. KBI's Consistency Advantage

KBI's 0.8% standard deviation is 4× tighter than MVP (3.2%) and 5× tighter than Genesys (4.1%). This consistency enables:

  • Predictable profitability (easier forecasting)
  • Confidence for investors and lenders
  • Ability to scale without surprise margin drops

3. The Strategy-Margin Correlation

Formula-based vendors (KBI) have higher, more consistent margins.

  • KBI (Formula): 37.3% ±0.8% — formula-driven pricing
  • Sligo (Cost-Leader): 26.4% ±2.1% — volume-focused
  • Genesys (Reactive): 24.7% ±4.1% — unpredictable ad-hoc

4. Market Share vs Financial Dominance

KBI has 21.3% of wins but likely ~31% of total market revenue (estimate) because their higher margins drive higher contract values. Margin advantage = market power.

5. V2.0 Competitive Positioning

By adopting KBI's formula, V2.0 achieves:
✓ Highest margins of any non-KBI model (matching KBI at 37.3%) ✓ Best consistency across all vendors (0.8% std dev) ✓ Scalable profitability (formula-based growth) ✓ Defensible positioning (proven by 317 wins)

Vendor Strategy Profiles

KBI: The Formula Winner

Strategy: Fixed 110% markup applied uniformly across all roles/regions. Result: Predictable 37.3% margins enable compound growth and market dominance.

MVP: The Balanced Player

Strategy: Opportunistic pricing with selective margin harvesting. Result: Second-best margins (31.8%) but with higher variance (3.2%).

Sligo: The Cost Leader

Strategy: Volume-focused, moderate margins, good consistency. Result: Lower margins (26.4%) but predictable, scalable via volume.

Panha: The Aggressive Bidder

Strategy: Price-to-win, aggressive bidding, minimum margins. Result: Lowest margins (23.1%), requires high volume for viability.

Genesys: The Inconsistent Player

Strategy: Reactive pricing with no clear formula. Result: Scattered margins (24.7% ±4.1%), hardest to predict outcomes.
