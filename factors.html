What Drives Each Formula — Factors, Variables & Parameters

A reference of every input behind Formula 1.0 and Formula 1.1: the outside forces (factors), the inputs that change per calculation (variables), and the settings we choose (parameters) — with how each one pushes the price up or down.

FACTOR An outside force we don't control (inflation, competitors, the buyer's rules).
VARIABLE An input that changes for each calculation (role, region, a competitor's rate).
PARAMETER A setting we choose and can tune (markup, target rank, cost assumptions).

Effect column: ↑ raises our price · ↓ lowers it · ↕ depends. Control: market / fixed / we tune.

🧮 Formula 1.0 — Baseline (cost-plus "recipe")

Bill = Wage × (1 + Markup/100) · Wage = Historical 2021 rate × CPI multipliers
Price is built from our own costs escalated by inflation. It does not look at competitors.

📍 Formula 1.1 — Rank Positioning ("line-up")

Pick price so we are #TargetRank cheapest: c(18) < OurPrice < c(19) → rank 19 of 33
Price is set by where competitors sit. Our own costs only decide the resulting margin, not the price.
InputTypeWhat it isEffectControl
